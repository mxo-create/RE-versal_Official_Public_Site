An initiative of RE-versal · Digital Environmental Intelligence
Pilot Phase

UniClean

Turning environmental challenges into actionable intelligence.

UniClean is a digital environmental reporting platform designed to improve how communities, organisations, and institutions identify, report, and respond to environmental challenges.

By combining location-based reporting with structured data collection, UniClean transforms isolated reports into reliable evidence that supports better decision-making, stronger collaboration, and more effective environmental action.

Current Focus
  • Internal pilot implementation
  • Platform refinement
  • Environmental data collection
  • Partnership development
  • Community validation
An initiative of RE-versal · Research & Collaboration
Research & Network Development

Regenerative Systems Network

Connecting knowledge. Strengthening collaboration. Inspiring action.

The Regenerative Systems Network is a collaborative research and knowledge initiative that explores how organisations, innovators, researchers, and communities are responding to today's interconnected social, environmental, and economic challenges.

The network brings together diverse perspectives to identify emerging opportunities, encourage collaboration, and generate practical insights that support regenerative and sustainable development.

Our current objective is to collect at least 100 responses from across different regions to inform future dialogue, partnerships, research, and collaborative action.

Current Focus
  • Global data collection
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Research and insight generation
  • Partnership development
  • Building a collaborative innovation network
